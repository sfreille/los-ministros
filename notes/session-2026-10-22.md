## Intended result

A polished, informative, locally testable HTML visualization built with D3, for students and a wider audience of political scientists, economic historians, and economists. No hosting or publication has been requested. Prefer a portable HTML artifact, with the library and data bundled for offline use if feasible.

The user's three questions:

1. Average duration of economy/finance ministers per president.
2. Total number of economy/finance ministers per president.
3. Differences across political regimes, including full democracy, restricted democracy, and military government/dictatorship.

## Proposed roadmap — awaiting approval

### 1. Audit data and define measures

- Inspect every relevant CSV and document coverage, missing dates, duplicate appointments, ongoing spells, overlaps, and president-link discrepancies.
- Preserve all existing CSVs and scraped HTML inputs. Write any derived data separately, with reproducible transformation code and an audit log.
- Use presidential terms as the comparison unit; do not merge separate terms merely because the president has the same name.
- Distinguish unique people, appointment spells, and ministers serving during a term, including inherited incumbents.
- For presidential comparisons, calculate each minister's service within that presidential term by intersecting date intervals. Show appointments initiated during the term separately from the number of ministers who served during it.
- Report term length alongside counts and an appointments-per-year measure. Explain that short presidencies can produce extreme annualized values.
- Define a fixed dataset cutoff. Label ongoing spells as observed-to-cutoff rather than completed tenures; allow completed-spell comparisons without treating ongoing observations as final durations.
- Keep full appointment duration available separately from service within a presidential term.

Deliverable: a short data/measurement note and derived data suitable for the prototype.

### 2. Build the first local D3 prototype

- Presidential comparison: linked counts and average service within term, chronological and ranked ordering, and a count/rate toggle with explicit denominators.
- Historical timeline: ministerial spells alongside presidential terms, date-range selection/zoom, and selection details.
- Linked interactions: selecting a president narrows or highlights relevant records in the other view; provide clear/reset controls and name search.
- Show exact dates, original office labels, duration definitions, and data-quality flags in a detail panel.
- Bundle a local HTML artifact under `output/`, keeping the existing Plotly outputs intact. Keep implementation/build sources under `code/`.

Deliverable: a first HTML prototype to open and test locally with the user.

### 3. Develop and document the regime comparison

- Establish a dated classification with explicit criteria and cited historical sources. Constitutional status alone does not establish full democracy.
- Address suffrage restrictions, electoral fraud, party proscription, and military rule explicitly. Avoid silently applying present-day categories to all periods.
- Keep the classification in an editable derived lookup table with dates, rationale, and sources. Discuss consequential classification choices with the user.
- Initially, any view based on the existing CSV regime labels must be labeled provisional.
- Compare individual observations and distributions, medians, means, and sample sizes; show ongoing observations distinctly.
- Define how appointments spanning regime changes enter comparisons; avoid duplicating a full tenure across regimes. Disclose unequal observation periods and avoid causal claims from descriptive differences.

Deliverable: a linked regime comparison plus a visible classification/methodology note.

### 4. Refine for teaching and public exploration

- Add a small number of guided period presets and concise, sourced historical annotations.
- Add downloadable filtered records and definitions for every metric.
- Refine responsive layout, keyboard access, readable color/label combinations, reduced-motion behavior, empty states, and reset behavior.

### 5. Validate and hand off

- Check derived counts and durations against selected source records, especially presidential transition dates, repeat presidents/ministers, short terms, missing end dates, and ongoing spells.
- Verify that filters, rankings, timeline selections, and downloads agree.
- Preview in a browser at desktop and narrow widths; check console errors and offline behavior if the artifact is bundled.
- Document the exact rebuild command, data cutoff, limitations, and output location. Report any verification that could not be performed.
